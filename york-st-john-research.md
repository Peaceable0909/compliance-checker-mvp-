# York St John University — verified requirements research

Research date: 2026-08-13. Sources are official York St John University pages.

## Nigeria-specific academic and English requirements

Source: https://www.yorksj.ac.uk/international/how-to-apply/entry-requirements-for-your-country-or-region/

- Foundation degree: West African Senior School Certificate (WAEC), minimum 5 credit (C6) passes, plus University Matriculation Examination minimum 350.
- Undergraduate degree: completed Year 1 (level 100) of a degree course at a recognised Nigerian university, GPA 2.6 or higher.
- OND/ND: minimum lower credit pass.
- Recognised International Foundation Programme: pass on all modules.
- Postgraduate degree: Bachelor Degree from a recognised university; 50 to 70 or Second Class (lower credit) to First Class depending on course.
- Some postgraduate courses require a related subject; individual course pages govern this.
- Qualifications obtained in the Republic of Benin are not accepted.
- Undergraduate English: WAEC/NECO English C6 or above; only WAEC/NECO dated 2005 or newer; scratch-card verification may be requested.
- Postgraduate English: IELTS 6.0 with no skill below 5.5 or equivalent, including WAEC/NECO English C6 or above; scratch-card verification may be requested.

## General international document checklist

Source: https://www.yorksj.ac.uk/international/how-to-apply/apply-direct/

- Personal statement.
- Clear scans of official qualifications, including certificates and transcripts where appropriate, translated into English where necessary.
- Proof of English language level.
- Academic references, signed, dated, stamped, and on official letterhead.
- Passport photo page and any previous UK visas.
- CV.

## General English baseline

Source: https://www.yorksj.ac.uk/international/how-to-apply/english-language-requirements/

- For most courses: IELTS Academic 6.0 overall, minimum 5.5 in each component, or equivalent.
- September 2026 published exceptions include BA Journalism and BA Sports Journalism: IELTS 7.5 with no less than 7.5 in each component; MA TESOL: IELTS 6.5 with no less than 6.0; MSc Speech and Language Therapy (Pre-Registration): IELTS 7.0 with no less than 6.5; MRes courses: IELTS 6.5 with no less than 6.0 except MRes Business with 6.5 Writing; PhD: IELTS 7.0 with no less than 6.0; Professional Doctorate: IELTS 6.0 with no less than 5.5.

## CAS / visa-stage documents

Source: https://www.yorksj.ac.uk/international/international-offer-holders/preparing-for-cas/

- Original valid passport.
- Final academic transcripts and diplomas.
- English qualification where applicable.
- Financial evidence when requested, including tuition and living costs; York campus published maintenance figure is £1,136 per month for 9 months (£10,224), London campus £1,483 per month for 9 months (£13,347).
- Parent/guardian funding requires a sponsor letter and original birth certificate showing relationship, with translation if needed.
- Financial evidence must generally show funds for at least 28 consecutive days; bank statement no more than 31 days old when using parent/guardian funds.
- Deposit: degree courses generally require 50% of first-year tuition fees, subject to exemptions and the offer letter.

## Implementation caution

The university publishes country-level and English requirements clearly, but it does not publish a single universal document set for every programme. The rules engine should therefore add verified baseline rules and programme-specific English thresholds, while leaving programme-specific academic subject requirements configurable per course rather than inventing them.
